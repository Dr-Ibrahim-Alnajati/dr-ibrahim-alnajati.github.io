About

I am an academic in mechanical engineering with a PhD from Universiti Sains Malaysia (2024), specialising in energy. My research focuses on harvesting energy from piezoelectric materials, from pavements and from tire strain, supported by finite-element and multiphysics modelling.

I combine teaching, supervision of graduation projects and academic administration at a technical engineering college, and I share educational videos for students on YouTube.

Research interests

Piezoelectric (PZT) energy harvesting
Energy from pavements and tire strain
Sustainable asphalt mixtures and pavement dynamics
Multiphysics finite-element modelling (COMSOL)
Asphalt pavement health monitoring

Research

Four connected lines of work.

Energy

Tire-strain energy harvesting

Piezoelectric harvesters embedded in tires to power on-board sensing, from design and simulation to experimental validation.

Infrastructure

Pavement energy and monitoring

Harvesting energy from asphalt pavements and using it for pavement health monitoring.

Modelling

Multiphysics simulation

Finite-element models in COMSOL Multiphysics to optimise devices before building them.

Materials

Sustainable construction materials

Thermal insulation and building materials made from local and recycled resources.
